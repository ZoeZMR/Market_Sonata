05
Method

How a chart becomes a song

i

Read the market

Daily, hourly or monthly bars from Yahoo Finance are cut into twelve chapters. Each chapter gets a shape, a momentum, a volatility and a level of participation.

ii

Find the mood

The overall trend chooses the modal family — bright, dark or ambiguous. Volatility sets the tempo and how dissonant the chords get; volume sets how hard the band plays.

iii

Write a song

A four-note motif from the first chapter is developed across Intro · Verse · Chorus · Bridge · Outro. The chorus hook comes from the market's most frenzied stretch.

iv

Sign it

The ticker itself is hashed into a signature — key, groove, bass figure, drum kit, swing — so two assets with similar charts still sound like different songs.

The market does……the music does
Rises / fallsMelody climbs / descends; bright vs. dark modes
Gets volatileFaster tempo, denser rhythm, 7th chords, harder drums
Trades heavilyLouder dynamics, fuller texture
Hits its most energetic stretchThat stretch becomes the chorus hook
